MoE Ethiopian National Curriculum Standard Analysis

Below is the structured topic breakdown aligned with Ministry of Education standards for Grade 6 assessments.

Unit 1: Whole Numbers & Operations: Place value, prime factorization, LCM, and HCF.

Unit 2: Fractions and Decimals: Conversion, simplification, mixed arithmetic, and estimation.

Unit 3: Ratios, Proportions & Percentages: Real-world rate calculations and percentage conversions.

Unit 4: Linear Equations: Basic single-variable equations and word problems.

Unit 5: Perimeter, Area & Volume: Area of composite 2D shapes, surface area of simple solids.

Unit 6: Data Handling & Probability: Reading bar graphs, pie charts, mean, median, and mode.

Unit 1: Natural Environment of Ethiopia: Vegetation zones, wildlife conservation, national parks.

Unit 2: Human Body Systems: Digestive, circulatory, and respiratory system structures.

Unit 3: Energy Types and Transformations: Solar, thermal, mechanical, and electrical energy.

Unit 4: Ecosystems & Conservation: Food chains, deforestation impacts, and soil conservation.

Unit 5: Weather & Climate: Ethiopian seasonal patterns, instruments, and climate impact on agriculture.

Units 1-3: Grammar & Usage: Tenses (Present Perfect, Simple Past), modal verbs, adverbs.

Units 4-6: Reading Comprehension: Expository texts regarding culture, history, and health.

Units 7-10: Vocabulary & Writing: Contextual clues, paragraph construction, formal letters.
